What does confidence level mean?

Confidence represents how strongly you believe your investment thesis is correct based on your research and current evidence.

Use a lower percentage when your thesis needs more evidence, and a higher percentage when your reasoning is well supported. This is a reflection tool, not a guarantee of performance.
